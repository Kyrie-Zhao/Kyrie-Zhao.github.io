Building AI around people.

I’m the founder and CEO of AxiomsTen, building Spiro. My work explores how AI can understand a person over time—and use that understanding to be helpful at the right moment.

I earned my PhD at The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2025, researching efficient AI systems. Previously, I co-founded ThingX and helped build Nuna, originally introduced as PieX, and Collie R1. Those experiences brought me from making AI work on devices to asking how it can work better for people.

01Current work

Spiro

Building at AxiomsTen

What would it take for AI to understand you beyond a single conversation?

People change. Their priorities shift, relationships evolve, and the same words can mean different things at different times. A useful personal AI needs more than a longer conversation history.

At Spiro, I’m exploring how everyday experiences can become an evolving understanding of a person: grounded in evidence, open to correction, and useful for a specific task. The goal is to help AI decide what matters now, what context to use, and when to leave someone alone.

Understand over time

Connect experiences without turning a passing moment into a permanent label.

Act with context

Use the relevant understanding for the task, with permission and attention to timing.

Learn from correction

Let new evidence and the person’s own feedback change the system’s view.

An ongoing research and product effort, informed by our earlier work on wearable AI and personal memory.

The direction I’m working toward

Make the person the operating system for AI.
